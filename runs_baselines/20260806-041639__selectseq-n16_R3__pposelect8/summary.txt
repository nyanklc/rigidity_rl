WHAT THE METHODS ARE
  initial   the random graph each method starts from
  random    uniform random actions - the floor any method should beat
  learned   the trained policy

WHAT THE COLUMNS MEAN
  edges     how many directed bearing measurements the final network needs.
            Each edge is a sensor/communication link, so fewer is better.
  score     the objective every method is scored with (phi). Higher is better;
            it rewards rigidity and penalises each extra edge.
  rigid     % of networks whose shape is fully determined by its bearing
            measurements. This is the property being solved for.
  minimal   % that are rigid AND use the fewest possible edges.
            (heuristic on mixed-domain networks - may under-report)
  rigidity  how much margin the network has before it stops being rigid.
            Larger is more robust; ~1e-5 is normal at this scale.
  work      how many changes to the network the method actually made.
  best@     the step at which its best network was found. Lower means it
            converged faster; the rest of the budget added nothing.

HOW TO READ IT
  'initial' and 'optimal' are reference points, not competing methods:
  every method starts from 'initial', and 'optimal' is the best achievable.
  A method is doing well when it approaches 'optimal' with low 'work'.
  All methods are run on the same networks, so rows compare directly.
